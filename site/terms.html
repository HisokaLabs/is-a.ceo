The service

{{org_name}} operates the DNS zones listed on this site and delegates a subdomain to anyone who follows the registration process. The service is free, has no uptime guarantee, and is run by volunteers on donated infrastructure and automation. There is no contract of sale and no paid support tier.

Who can claim a name

  • One person or one project per subdomain, claimed through a pull request against the registry.
  • You must be able to hold the record you are asking for and be reachable at the contact address in your entry.
  • Names are first come, first served. A merge is the only thing that reserves a name.
  • Names that impersonate a person, a brand, or a public body are refused, and refused again if resubmitted.

What you may not do

  • Phishing, malware delivery, credential harvesting, or command-and-control hosting.
  • Spam, bulk unsolicited mail, or anything that gets the zone onto a blocklist.
  • Content that is illegal in {{jurisdiction}} or at the point where it is consumed.
  • Reselling subdomains, using them as a redirect farm, or claiming names to sit on them.
  • Pointing a record at infrastructure you do not control.

The zones are DNS zones. Records can be removed at any time, without notice, if a name breaks these terms. That is the entire enforcement mechanism: removal of the DNS entry. See the abuse policy for how a report is handled.

What you keep

You keep ownership of your content, your code, and your project. You grant {{org_name}} nothing beyond the right to publish the record data you submit in the public registry. The registry itself is public, including the contact details you put in your entry; do not put a private address in it.

No warranty

The service is provided as is, with no warranty of availability, resolution, propagation, or fitness for any purpose. Records can break because of an upstream resolver, a registry automation failure, or a mistake in the entry. You are responsible for keeping a copy of anything you cannot afford to lose, and for having a real domain ready if a subdomain is not enough.

Limitation of liability

To the extent permitted by law, {{org_name}} is not liable for any loss arising from the use of a subdomain, from a record not resolving, or from a name being removed. If a court finds that liability cannot be excluded, it is limited to the amount you paid for the service, which is zero.

Changes and termination

  • These terms can change. The current version is always this page.
  • A zone can be discontinued, migrated, or handed to another operator.
  • Your entry can be removed if it breaks these terms or becomes unreachable for a long period.
  • You can remove your own entry at any time by deleting it in the registry.
