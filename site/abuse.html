Policy

Abuse policy

These zones are DNS zones. What is hosted at a subdomain is the responsibility of whoever claimed it. If something under any of them is harming you, here is exactly who to contact and what to expect.

Last updated {{year}}.

Report it

Email {{contact_email}} with the subject line abuse: <full hostname>, for example abuse: bad-actor.{{zone_example}}. Include:

  • The full hostname, zone included, e.g. bad-actor.{{zone_example}}.
  • What you observed: phishing page, malware delivery, illegal content, spam.
  • Evidence: URLs, screenshots, headers, a timeline. The more concrete, the faster it moves.
  • Your contact address, in case we need to follow up.

Reports in English or Indonesian are both fine. Encrypted mail is welcome: ask for the key and one will be provided.

What happens next

  1. Acknowledged within 48 hours. You get a reply confirming receipt, even if we cannot share details.
  2. Triage. We check who owns the subdomain from the registry and what the DNS actually resolves to.
  3. Action. Depending on the finding, we may contact the owner, remove the DNS records, or hand the case to the host or registrar involved.
  4. Resolution. You are told what action was taken, unless doing so would compromise another report or an ongoing investigation.

Removing DNS records does not delete hosted content, and it is not a legal remedy. For content that is illegal in your jurisdiction, report it to your local authority as well; we cooperate with lawful requests.

What gets a subdomain removed

  • Malware, phishing, or credential harvesting.
  • Distribution of illegal content.
  • Spam operations, including anything that gets the zone blocklisted.
  • Impersonating a brand, a public figure, or another person.
  • Anything that puts the whole zone, and every other subdomain in it, at risk of delisting.

Jurisdiction

The zones are operated from {{jurisdiction}} by {{org_name}}. Content hosted at a subdomain is expected to be legal there and at the point of use. The registry's terms are in CONTRIBUTING.md.

If it is not abuse

Broken DNS, wrong records, or a subdomain that should be renamed: that is a bug, not abuse. Use the issue tracker instead: it gets fixed faster.
